# City Care Hospital: patient information

These are the only facts the info agent may use. If a question isn't answered here, the agent says it doesn't know and offers to connect the patient to staff.

## Address and contact

- City Care Hospital, 12 MG Road, Camp, Pune 411001.
- Front desk phone: 020-4000-1234 (Monday to Saturday, 9am to 8pm).
- In an emergency, call **108** or come straight to our 24-hour Emergency department at the main entrance.

## OPD (outpatient) hours

- Monday to Saturday: 10am to 1pm and 5pm to 8pm.
- Closed on Sundays and on public holidays.
- Appointments are 30 minutes long.

## Departments and doctors

- Dermatology (skin): Dr. Anjali Mehta, Dr. Vikram Rao
- General Medicine: Dr. Sunita Iyer, Dr. Rahul Sharma
- Orthopaedics (bones and joints): Dr. Imran Khan
- Paediatrics (children): Dr. Maria Fernandes

## Fees and payment

- Consultation fee: Rs 500 for every doctor, paid at the counter or by payment link.
- We accept cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards.
- Payment links sent on WhatsApp are valid for 24 hours.

## Before your visit

- Bring a photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence) and any old reports or prescriptions.
- Arrive 15 minutes early for your first visit to register.
- For children, a parent or guardian must come along.

## Changing or cancelling

- You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 4 hours before your appointment.
- Within 4 hours of the appointment, please call the front desk or ask to speak to our staff.
- If you miss your appointment, you'll need to book a new one.

## Facilities

- Free parking in the basement for patients (2 hours).
- Pharmacy on the ground floor, open 8am to 10pm every day.
- Cafeteria on the first floor, open 8am to 8pm.
- Wheelchairs are available at the main entrance.

## Lab tests

- Sample collection: Monday to Saturday, 8am to 12pm.
- Fasting tests: no food for 10 to 12 hours before; water is fine.
- Most reports are ready within 24 hours and are sent on WhatsApp.
